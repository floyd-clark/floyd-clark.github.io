05 · Zero Trust grounding

A decision model inspired by the Zero Trust Exchange—not a claim of Zscaler telemetry.

The analogy uses Zscaler’s official operating sequence and ZDX’s end-to-end observability model: verify context, understand the destination, assess risk, enforce policy, observe the full path, and learn from anomalies.

Architecture reference only. This lab has no access to Zscaler customer, service-edge, device, network, application, or production incident data.

      1. I normalized 52 active market journeys across 23 anonymized organizations without publishing names, requisitions, contacts, or private evidence.
      2. I separated observed tracker facts, derived health metrics, forecasts, and hypotheses.
      3. I froze the forecast replay at the maximum LinkedIn signal peak: Sep 19, 2026 at 16:38 UTC.
      4. I used visible heuristics—stage, source reliability, recency, signal strength, coverage, and contradiction penalty—to derive decisions.
      5. I grounded the operating sequence in Zero Trust Exchange and ZDX concepts without implying access to production telemetry.
      6. I converted thresholds into actions: 18 follow-ups due, seven stale journeys, and one status contradiction to investigate.
      7. I recorded later events as outcomes; one replay forecast hit its window, while calibration remains statistically immature.
      8. The scale is personal, but the operating pattern is durable: establish truth, expose risk, facilitate decisions, verify outcomes, and improve the next version.
