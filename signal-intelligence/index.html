      Interview mode

      90-second guided tour

      1. I started with fragmented signals and preserved their source context.
      2. I normalized them into a governed event and evidence model.
      3. I defined health indicators and thresholds that change decisions.
      4. I separated observed facts, derived metrics, forecasts, and hypotheses.
      5. I used AI to accelerate extraction and synthesis without promoting model output to fact.
      6. I froze forecasts before outcomes, then verified them against what actually happened.
      7. Misses become inputs to the next model or process version.
      8. The scale here is small; the operating pattern is the same one I use to establish truth, expose risk, facilitate decisions, and verify outcomes in complex technical programs.
