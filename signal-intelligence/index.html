Signal Intelligence is a small, inspectable model for tracing an observation to evidence, testing competing explanations, choosing an action, and checking what happened next.

THE OPERATING LOOP

01 / THE METHOD

A claim needs a trail.

The model keeps source facts separate from interpretation. A stronger signal can change an action; a contradiction can pause one.

01

Record the source

Capture what was observed, when, where it came from, and a stable evidence ID. Missing provenance stays visible.

Scope & limitations

All scenarios and quantities are synthetic. Confidence labels are qualitative decision aids; they are not probabilities or claims about production service reliability. The source is public for inspection. Reuse terms are described in the repository README.
