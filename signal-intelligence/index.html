    04 · Conclusion heuristics

    How the system moves from evidence to a decision.

    The model is intentionally qualitative. It exposes the rules that raise, cap, or withhold confidence instead of hiding them behind an unsupported precision score.

    Strategic power principles

    Power is optionality, scarcity, and control of the decision system.

    The page uses power as disciplined agency—not manipulation: create multiple credible paths, compound capabilities that are hard to replace, own the operating mechanisms, and protect downside when evidence is incomplete.

    05 · Zero Trust grounding

    A decision model inspired by the Zero Trust Exchange—not a claim of Zscaler telemetry.

    The analogy uses Zscaler’s official operating sequence and ZDX’s end-to-end observability model: verify context, understand the destination, assess risk, enforce policy, observe the full path, and learn from anomalies.

    Architecture reference only. This lab has no access to Zscaler customer, service-edge, device, network, application, or production incident data.
