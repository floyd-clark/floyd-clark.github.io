04 / FORECAST LEDGER

State the next event.
Check what happened.

Prospective checks based on the redacted tracker. The window and verification rule are fixed at publication; outcomes are recorded later.

2032 scenario lens

The private decision model compares career paths from a 2026 index of 100. The public view pools broad paths and hides company-level options. These ranges represent modeled possibilities, not chances of an offer or a financial promise.

No calibrated probabilities: the tracker does not contain a reliable historical conversion series. A future outcome must be supported by a dated direct response or stage record; LinkedIn impressions alone cannot verify it.

05 / EVIDENCE MODEL

Claim only what the sources support.

The product keeps observed records, derived summaries, forecasts, and hypotheses distinct. A later outcome updates the record; it never rewrites the original forecast.

NO IMPLIED CERTAINTY
Public data contract

Published: aggregate counts, broad role families, stage buckets, age bands, and weekly LinkedIn metrics. Excluded: company names, exact role titles, requisition IDs, contacts, private links, emails, and raw tracker notes. This is a personal decision-system demonstration, not production telemetry or a calibrated prediction model.
