Choose a cohort and an assumed daily response rate. This rate is an input for exploration, not an estimate learned from the tracker.

Model: E(t) = N × [1 − (1 − q)t]. N = cohort size; q = assumed daily response rate per role; t = days since Sep 26. Curves use ½q, q, and 2q. Independent daily opportunities and constant q are illustrative assumptions.

2032 scenario lens

The private decision model compares career paths from a 2026 index of 100. The public view pools broad paths and hides company-level options. These ranges represent modeled possibilities, not chances of an offer or a financial promise.

Illustrative interpolation: I(y) = 100 × (I2032 / 100)(y − 2026) / 6. Endpoints are rounded, pooled ranges from the private model; intervening years are calculated paths, not observed results or annual forecasts.

No calibrated probabilities: the tracker does not contain a reliable historical conversion series. A future outcome must be supported by a dated direct response or stage record; LinkedIn impressions alone cannot verify it.
